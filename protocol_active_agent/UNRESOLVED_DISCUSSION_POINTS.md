### 1.1 Blockchain Network Selection
**Discussion**: Base vs Optimism vs Arbitrum vs Polygon zkEVM
**Current State**: Listed multiple options, no final decision
**Considerations**:
- Base: Coinbase backing, low fees, but newer
- Optimism: Mature, high throughput, established ecosystem
- Arbitrum: Largest L2, but higher fees
- Polygon zkEVM: Native ZK, but smaller ecosystem

**Action Needed**: Finalize network choice based on pilot requirements

**Discussion**: Circom vs Halo2 vs Nova
**Current State**: Circom 2.1.4 specified, but Halo2 mentioned for cost optimization
**Considerations**:
- Circom: Mature, good tooling, but higher gas costs
- Halo2: More efficient, but newer and less tested
- Nova: Recursive proofs, but complex implementation

**Action Needed**: Performance testing and cost-benefit analysis
